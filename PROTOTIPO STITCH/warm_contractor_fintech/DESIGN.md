## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for trade contractors, field specialists, and modern construction crews who manage cash flow, material disbursements, and client invoicing on the go. The aesthetic pairs approachable warmth with fintech confidence: inviting, tactile, and ergonomically generous.

The emotional core is stress-free clarity and trustworthy warmth. Moving away from rigid, utilitarian enterprise tools, this design system combines warm-tinted neutral surfaces, friendly rounded contours, and sleek glassmorphic elevation to make financial workflows feel effortless under variable on-site conditions.

Key stylistic pillars:
- **Warm & Approachable Modernism**: Soft, welcoming geometry combined with human, open typography that eliminates intimidation from numbers and balances.
- **Diffused Soft Depth**: Translucent frosted panels, airy atmospheric drop shadows, and delicate low-contrast rim lighting rather than harsh dividing lines.
- **Ergonomic Tactility**: Fluid pill-shaped indicators, generous 20–24px surface radiuses, and frictionless bottom-screen interaction zones designed for active one-handed operation.

## Colors

The color palette centers on vibrant cobalt blue, balanced by refreshing modern emerald and bathed in soft, warm-tinted neutral grays.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Fresh cobalt blue drives focal actions, key transactional triggers, active navigation markers, and verified fiscal statuses.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: A modern emerald accent reserved for positive revenue flow, collected payments, successful milestone validations, and completed inspections.
- **Tertiary (`#1D4ED8`)**: Deep cobalt anchor tone used for pressed button states, dense headline emphasis, and high-priority dashboard cards.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#F8FAFC`)**: A gentle, warm-tinted slate neutral providing a soft visual plane without eye-straining glare.
- **Semantic Accents**: Critical notices, outgoing expenses, and overdue invoices leverage warm coral red (`#EF4444`). Pure `#000000` is avoided in favor of deep slate (`#0F172A`) for high contrast with natural warmth.

## Typography

The typography is built entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, imparting a soft, geometric, and human presence throughout the product.

### Numerical Data & Accounting Formats
- Financial totals, metric dashboards, and line-item sums enforce tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`) to preserve column rhythm.
- Large currency indicators use semi-bold and bold weights with ample line heights to prevent descender clipping on mobile viewports.

### Reading Hierarchy & Microcopy
- Labels, secondary stats, and timestamp indicators avoid dense capitalization; sentence case with generous tracking (`0.01em`) maintains an approachable tone.
- Critical field values leverage high font weights (`600`–`700`) to remain legible at brief glances in bright light.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid grid system tailored for fluid thumb reach and glanceable mobile dashboards.

- **Mobile Viewport (360px – 599px)**: 4-column layout with `16px` outer canvas margins and `12px` gutters. Card components group project logs, invoices, and job metrics into cohesive vertical streams.
- **Tablet / Vehicle Mount (600px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters. Supports split-screen layouts: active job tickets on the primary column and financial summaries in the secondary panel.
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout centered with `32px` margins and max content width of `1200px`.
- **Thumb Reach Rhythm**: Essential actionable elements (drawers, rapid-log floating actions, payment verifications) live in the bottom screen envelope with 16–24px bottom buffer spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines warm tonal surfaces, frosted glassmorphism, and soft, diffused ambient drop shadows—avoiding sharp or heavy dark borders.

- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC`. The warm neutral foundation.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Bars**: Translucent top sheets and floating bottom nav bars use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ambient inner highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`.
- **Ambient Shadow (Cards & Tiles)**: Multi-stage, extra-diffused warm tint shadow without harsh perimeter lines: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Elevated Interactive Sheets**: Bottom modals and invoice approval sheets employ `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Lighting**: Card containers use pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC`, relying on ambient shadow and soft tonal shift rather than distinct 1px outlines.

## Shapes

The system adopts a friendly, soft geometry featuring generous curves:

- **Large Components & Main Cards**: Built with `1.5rem` (`24px`) corner radii (`ROUND_TWENTY_FOUR`), delivering soft, organic surfaces that feel modern and approachable.
- **Standard Controls & Modals**: Action buttons, input containers, and micro-sheets utilize `1rem` (`16px`) corner radii (`ROUND_SIXTEEN`).
- **Pill Badges & Chips**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) for status indicators, dynamic filters, and category badges to soften density across lists.
- **Sheet Drawers**: Slide-up modal tops feature `24px` rounded upper corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#2563EB` solid fill, white text, `16px` border-radius, minimum height 52px. Hover/active state deepens to `#1D4ED8` with a soft scale press transform (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary CTA**: Translucent cobalt tint (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10)`) with `#2563EB` text and `16px` border-radius.
- **Floating Action Buttons**: Pill-shaped or circle (`56px` height) with soft diffused drop shadow (`0px 12px 28px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.28)`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Pill Status Badges**: Fully rounded (`9999px`), `28px` to `32px` height, featuring a soft pastel background with saturated foreground text.
  - *Paid / Inflow*: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` background with `#047857` text.
  - *Pending / Active*: `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)` background with `#1D4ED8` text.
  - *Overdue / Outgoing*: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)` background with `#B91C1C` text.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- **Overview Cards**: `#FFFFFF` background with `24px` border-radius, cushioned with `20px` internal padding and diffused soft shadow.
- **Invoice & Job Item Cards**: Warm white cards with an emerald or cobalt accent dot, rounded corners (`20px`), and soft metadata grouping.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Fields**: Height 52px, `#FFFFFF` background with subtle fill tint, `16px` radius, and 16px horizontal padding. Active focus features a soft cobalt halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.18)`), avoiding hard borders.
- **Numeric Entry Display**: Prominent centered typography in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, set within a frosted pill or soft 24px tile.

### Lists & Transaction Feeds
- **Feed Items**: Contained in soft individual cards (`16px` radius) or seamlessly grouped with generous 14px row gutters, featuring round 44px icon tiles for category avatars.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Rounded checkbox tiles (`8px` corner radius) and smooth pill radio buttons. Checked state applies `#2563EB` fill with a smooth scale-in checkmark transition.
